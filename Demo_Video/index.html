About this visualization Source figure · animation semantics · controls

Source. The artwork is page 1 of the supplied Overview_fig.pptx. Its robots, panels and training scenes are preserved. Animated signals and selectors are separate SVG elements.

Illustration, not telemetry. The phase cycle, contact window and highlighted reference poses are presentation choreography. They do not assert measured timings, an exact deployed state machine, or an online motion-matching result. The robots in the source image are still poses. The supplied archive did not contain the referenced paper/LaTeX folder.

Controls. Click a task phase to pin it; Play resumes. Switch the followed player with the A/B button. Drag the timeline to inspect a moment. Pause freezes the entire animation; Reduce motion keeps a readable static diagram. Keyboard: Space, arrows and R.

Archify. The second view is rendered from the checked typed source using the official Archify package. The paper-artwork overlay is custom: this preserves the slide instead of forcing its robot illustrations into software-service cards.
